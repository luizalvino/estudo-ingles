# past-time Specification

## Purpose

Aula teórica de past time do básico ao uso real, com explicações em português, exemplos traduzidos e exercícios com correção, baseada na Unit 3 do livro.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Lesson teaches the past simple first
The system SHALL present the past simple form and its core uses (single completed actions, repeated/habitual actions, past states) before any other past tense.

#### Scenario: Student starts the lesson
- **WHEN** the student reads the first sections
- **THEN** the system shows the form, translated examples, and the emphatic use with did

### Requirement: Lesson contrasts past simple vs present perfect simple
The system SHALL contrast finished past periods (past simple) with periods continuing up to the present or with present relevance (present perfect simple), with paired examples.

#### Scenario: Student confuses the two tenses
- **WHEN** the student reads the comparison section
- **THEN** the system shows paired examples and states when each tense applies

### Requirement: Lesson covers past continuous uses
The system SHALL explain the past continuous for actions in progress at or around a past moment, temporary situations, changing situations, background events, and annoying past habits with always.

#### Scenario: Student studies the past continuous section
- **WHEN** the student reads the past continuous section
- **THEN** the system shows each use with a translated example

### Requirement: Lesson contrasts past simple vs past continuous
The system SHALL contrast background events in progress (past continuous) with main events (past simple) and repeated past actions (past simple).

#### Scenario: Student studies the contrast
- **WHEN** the student reads the past simple vs past continuous section
- **THEN** the system shows paired examples of background and main events

### Requirement: Lesson covers past perfect simple and continuous
The system SHALL explain the past perfect simple for situations/actions before a past moment and the past perfect continuous for actions continuing up to a past moment, with translated examples.

#### Scenario: Student reaches the past perfect sections
- **WHEN** the student reads the past perfect sections
- **THEN** the system shows the form and at least one translated example for each

### Requirement: Lesson covers past habits with used to and would
The system SHALL explain past habits with `used to` and `would`, including that `would` is not normally used for past states, and SHALL distinguish `used to` from `be used to`/`get used to`.

#### Scenario: Student reaches the habits section
- **WHEN** the student reads the habits section
- **THEN** the system shows the contrast between past habit and current familiarity with translated examples

### Requirement: Lesson includes exercises with answers
The system SHALL provide at least one exercise per level where the student can check the answer, plus a quick-treino section with timeable questions for the drill.

#### Scenario: Student practices
- **WHEN** the student completes an exercise and requests the answer
- **THEN** the system reveals the correct answer with a brief explanation in Portuguese
